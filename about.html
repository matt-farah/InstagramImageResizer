About

Hi! My name is Matt Farah and I am a photographer based out of Portland, OR.

I like posting images to Instagram, however I hate that your options are to either resize each image in your image editing software manually or upload images in an aspect ratio that they weren't initially shot in.

In spring of this year, I was looking for a tool to bulk resize images, center them, and maybe add a background color and I could not find one, so I built one.

Initially, this was a small Python script that lived on my desktop, but I wanted to have other be able to use this tool. So, I worked for the past few months to turn it into a website that respects your privacy -- this is important to me as a photographer and in my day job as a Security Engineer.

If you have any questions or want to see some of my work, check it out on Bluesky and Instagram.
